Paste rows from the sheet

Copy rows straight out of the sheet and paste them here — one trip per line, no header needed. If your sheet hides columns, untick them below: Excel leaves hidden columns out of what it copies, and nothing in the pasted text says so — read against the full layout, every value after the gap lands in the wrong column, plausibly enough to slip through validation. The ticked columns are read in order. (Remembered by this browser; a pasted header row, if one is included, overrides the ticks for that paste.)

Dates are read as the sheet renders them (1-Apr-26) or as 2026-04-01; anything ambiguous (04/05/2026) is kept as typed and reported by the contract. Rows that fail validation are still added — they appear under Non-operable with reasons, and one Undo removes the whole paste.
